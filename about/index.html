iOS Lead Essentials

Essential Developer, 2022

Hi, I'm Igor.

I lead iOS development at a bank, and I build my own products with AI coding agents. The agents work from specs and tests: I write down what a product must do, they write the code, and the tests decide when it is done.

What I'm building

  • RexLab, a dinosaur learning game for children aged 4 to 7.
  • FolioTell, an offline iPhone app that reads your notes aloud.
  • Agister, a runner for AI-agent work defined in settings files.
  • Agonara, a place to build learning programs with the agent you already use.

Each one is a public repository with its code and tests. The portfolio has screenshots and details.

Before the agents

Years of Swift and SwiftUI, written up as I went in the articles. In 2022 I completed iOS Lead Essentials at the Essential Developer Academy, and wrote down what I took from it.
